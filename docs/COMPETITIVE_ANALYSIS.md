# Competitive Landscape --- Initial Hypotheses

## Evidence level

A limited search found OpenMontage, an existing agentic video-production
project that describes production pipelines, tools, agent skills, and
Remotion composition. This is sufficient to reject the assumption that
"agentic video production" is an empty category, but not sufficient to
rank competitors or estimate market share.

## Initial differentiation hypotheses

RenderWeld should test whether buyers value: 1. First-class integration
of **both Remotion and Motion Canvas**. 2. Self-hosted/source-code
ownership and a clear deployment experience. 3. A shared, portable
project schema that can be planned by multiple agents. 4. Polished,
repeatable short-form templates rather than only general-purpose
pipelines. 5. Transparent provider costs, local execution, asset
provenance, and render QA.

These are hypotheses, not proven unique selling points.

## Competitor research tasks for an agent

-   Search GitHub, Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, and current
    product websites.
-   Include self-hosted/open-source and commercial products.
-   Compare capabilities: dual-engine support, agent planning, template
    quality, rendering, audio, subtitles, local models, BYO providers,
    deployment, license, pricing, and target users.
-   Cite primary sources and dated evidence.
-   Do not infer adoption from stars alone.
-   Do not claim "first," "only," or "unique" without strong evidence.
-   Record direct alternatives, adjacent tools, and substitutes
    (including traditional editors and hosted AI video tools).
-   Interview actual target users to understand which differentiators
    matter.
