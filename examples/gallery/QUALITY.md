## Review notes

Every contact sheet (12 frames per reel) was reviewed against the unslop signs list
(`.claude/skills/unslop/references/signs.md`) and the design rules in AGENTS.md. What the
review changed before the final render:

- All inline palettes moved to tinted neutrals with one accent; purple and electric-blue
  defaults replaced (Plum, Signal); headings at 600, sentence case, no tracked uppercase labels.
- Removed from every scene design: blurred colour blobs, radial washes, glows and drop shadows,
  gradient text and gradient bars, glass (backdrop-filter), conic gradients, pulsing rings and
  breathing loops, floating/swaying objects, overshoot easing, and the generative designs that
  were decoration only (aurora, glowing grid wave, liquid blob).
- The four built-in templates lost their glow, circles, blobs, ring and overshoot (see
  docs/GALLERY.md), and the 56 reels that use them were rendered again.
- Iris transitions covered whole scenes (a CSS order bug); fixed before the main render.
- A long word (a URL) overflowed the title card in two reels: title type now shrinks to fit.
- Multi-word brand names broke inside a word on end cards in four reels: letters are kept
  together per word and long names set smaller.
